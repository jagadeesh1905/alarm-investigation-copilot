# Pump Troubleshooting Guide (Demonstration Fixture)

Procedure ID: DEMO-PMP-001
Revision: 1
Status: SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION ONLY
Applicable asset types: Pump, Compressor

## High discharge pressure

When a high discharge pressure alarm is active, the operator should follow the approved site procedure, verify the displayed pressure using approved instrumentation, and review relevant operating conditions. Do not adjust process setpoints or isolate equipment unless the approved procedure and authorization permit it. Escalate persistent or worsening conditions to the responsible operator.

## High vibration

For a high vibration alarm, follow the approved inspection procedure and compare readings with site-defined limits. Review available trend information and escalate abnormal or increasing vibration to the reliability team. Do not touch moving equipment or bypass protective systems.
