# Motor Trip Response (Demonstration Fixture)

Procedure ID: DEMO-MTR-002
Revision: 1
Status: SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION ONLY
Applicable asset types: Motor
Applicable alarm: Motor trip

For a motor trip, follow the site-approved motor-trip response procedure. Review the trip indication and available operating history. Do not restart equipment until the required checks are complete and the responsible person has authorized restart. Escalate to the operations and electrical teams when the cause is unknown or the trip repeats.
